# Insights backlog

The queue for antedesign.be/insights. Roughly one post a week, worked top-down
within a cluster. Each entry: the claim, the search it answers, and the angle
that makes it Anté's rather than anyone's.

Rule of thumb for picking the next one: alternate clusters, so the blog does not
read as five posts about frameworks followed by five about bias. A news
interpretation (cluster F) can jump the queue when something actually happens.

---

## A. The models, and where they stop helping

The highest-traffic cluster. People search these terms constantly, and almost
everything written about them is a neutral summary. The angle is always the
same: explain it accurately, then say where it runs out.

| # | Claim | Target search | Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | ✅ **Published.** The Double Diamond is two decisions, not four phases | double diamond design process | Everything hangs on the two convergence points, and the model is silent on both |
| A2 | Jobs to be Done tells you why people switch, not what to build | jobs to be done framework | It is a lens on demand, not a specification. Teams read a JTBD statement as a brief and skip the solution diamond entirely |
| A3 | Desirability, feasibility, viability is a filter, not a poster | desirability feasibility viability | Three circles with no weighting and no threshold. Which one wins when they conflict is the actual decision, and the diagram does not say |
| A4 | Assumption mapping: rank what you do not know | assumption mapping workshop | Most maps sort by "importance". Sort by "how much the decision changes if this is wrong" instead, which is a different and more useful axis |
| A5 | The Business Model Canvas is a hypothesis sheet, not a summary | business model canvas | Filled in after the fact it describes. Filled in before, each box is a claim that can be tested. Same nine boxes, opposite value |
| A6 | RICE, ICE and the false precision of scoring models | rice prioritisation framework | Multiplying four guesses produces one number with four times the error and none of the visible doubt |
| A7 | Opportunity Solution Trees vs the Double Diamond | opportunity solution tree | Continuous discovery assumes a live product and a steady stream of users. Neither exists before the first build, which is where the model most often gets misapplied |
| A8 | The Kano model, and the misuse of "delighter" | kano model product | Categories are time-dependent. Yesterday's delighter is today's basic, so a Kano analysis has a shelf life nobody records |
| A9 | Wardley mapping without the vocabulary | wardley mapping explained | The useful half is the evolution axis. The rest is optional |

## F. News and interpretation

Not scheduled. Written when something real happens: a major platform launch, a
significant report, a regulation with product consequences, a large public
failure with a documented cause.

The format is fixed and short, 700 to 900 words:

1. What happened, in three sentences, with the primary source linked.
2. What most people will take from it.
3. What it actually changes for a team deciding what to build in the next
   quarter, which is usually less than the headline suggests and occasionally
   much more.
4. The one question to put on your own agenda this month.

Do not write one of these unless point 3 has a real answer. A news post with
nothing to change is filler, and filler is worse than silence.
