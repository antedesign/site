# Guardrails: what must never happen on antedesign.be

Every rule has a number so reviewers can cite it. A single violation is a NO-GO. When in
doubt, it is a violation. A skipped post costs nothing; a wrong post is public.

## A. Confidentiality (the firewall)

**R1. Nothing internal, ever.** No project, programme, partner, client, student, teacher,
colleague, meeting, document, number, hour, budget, plan or opinion that comes from anything
other than a public page you opened this run. This holds for the Anté internal repository,
the DesignWorks project, any university course, any interview or workshop, and anything
heard or read in a session, a note or a chat.

**R2. AIPPD, DesignWorks and PO-1 are never named, linked, hinted at or paraphrased.** Not
as a source, example, anecdote, event ("a launch event in Ghent"), partner list or thank-you.
No indirect version either: no "a design education project in Belgium", no "recently at an
industry event", no "students we spoke to". Anté's own claim about its work is limited to what
antedesign.be already says publicly.

**R3. No invented insider position.** Never write that Anté interviewed, tested, surveyed,
observed or worked with anyone unless that exact statement is already public on the site.
Write "in our reading" or "our view" for reasoning, and say nothing about experience.

## B. Sources, copyright and attribution

**R4. Every factual claim is traceable.** Author, year and a link in the same paragraph, to a
page you opened this run (and is in the research table). No "studies show", no "experts say",
no number without its source. Anté's own reasoning is labelled as such.

**R5. Words are ours.** No copied sentences. Quotation marks only around a verbatim passage of
at most 25 words that is in the research table, with its author named; at most 3 quotations in
a post. Paraphrase changes both words and structure. No translated passages. No eight-word run
copied from a source outside quotation marks.

**R6. No third-party material is reproduced.** No figure, chart, table of someone else's data,
screenshot, image, logo, code, dataset or full prompt copied from a source. Summarise a finding
in one sentence and link to it. Covers are generated by the build script and are the only images.
If a licence (CC BY or CC0) allowed reuse, still do not reuse it here.

**R7. Source quality.** Primary sources first: the paper, the publisher, the organisation that
made the thing. A preprint is called a preprint. Blogs, forums and Wikipedia are never the only
support for a claim. Never cite a page you did not open; never describe a paper beyond what you
read of it (abstract only means say abstract only).

**R8. Nothing invented.** No made-up number, date, quote, study, source, person, company, case,
testimonial or survey. An example that is invented is marked "illustrative" in the text.

## C. People, companies and claims

**R9. No private individuals, no personal data.** No names of private people, no contact
details, handles, photos, health or other personal information. Authors of cited public work may
be named for attribution. The only email address allowed is hello@antedesign.be.

**R10. Named companies, products and public figures: facts with a source, neutral words.** No
allegations, no disparagement, no ranking or recommendation of vendors, no affiliate or
sponsored language, no suggestion of partnership or endorsement. Names are used only to identify
the thing. Tool lists say "examples".

**R11. No advice in regulated areas.** No legal, medical, financial, tax or security advice.
Describing a rule (for example a regulation) means citing the primary text and saying it is not
legal advice.

**R12. No promises.** No guarantees, ROI claims, results for Anté, client names or logos, fake
urgency, superlatives about Anté, politics, religion or hype.

## D. Technical and safety

**R13. Stay inside your own files.** A post may create or change only: `posts-src/<slug>.*`,
`insights/<slug>/`, `assets/images/insights/<slug>.*`, and the generated lines in
`insights/index.html`, `sitemap.xml`, `.claude/skills/insights-post/QUEUE.md` and `TOPICS.md`.
Nothing else, ever. No deletions. No pushes to `main`.

**R14. No external resources and no secrets.** No third-party scripts, trackers, iframes, embeds,
hotlinked images or web fonts beyond what the site shell already loads. No keys, tokens, internal
URLs or file paths in any file.

**R15. The closing prompt is safe.** It never asks the reader to paste confidential, personal or
client data (it says the opposite), never contains instructions to bypass an AI's safeguards, and
contains no hidden instructions or links.

**R16. Honest status.** Say "preprint", "abstract only", "our view" and "illustrative" where they
apply. State dates explicitly, never "this year" or "recently".
