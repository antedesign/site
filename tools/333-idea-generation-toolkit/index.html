This toolkit will not invent your ideas for you. That is the point. It gives you prompts, pressure and structure, but the thinking stays yours. The same is true for AI: it can spark a direction, it cannot judge whether it is the right one. Treat both as a sparring partner, never as the decision maker.

Frame it as a "How might we..." question. It opens more doors than a statement.

Generate first, judge later. diverge

The best way to have a good idea is to have a lot of them. Set a timer, work in silence, and write one idea per line. Do not stop to evaluate. The obvious ideas come first, so get them out of the way fast. The interesting ones live just past the point where it starts to feel hard. Aim for 20.
