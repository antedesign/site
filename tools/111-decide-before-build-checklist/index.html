Where AI helps, and where it doesn't.

AI can accelerate almost everything in innovation. It still cannot replace judgment, empathy or real-world evidence. This checklist sorts the tasks in your project into three honest zones, so you use AI for leverage instead of false certainty. Your work saves automatically on this device.

This checklist follows its own rule. It does not make the call for you. It asks a few honest questions and shows you the consequence. The output is only as sharp as your answers, which is exactly the lesson: AI gives you confident output fast, but the moment you treat that confidence as evidence, you are building on synthetic certainty.
