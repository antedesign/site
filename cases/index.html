AI Product Check Book a call

Selected work.

Two products with AI at their centre: one physical, one digital. We share what each product does and for whom. The technology and our clients' input stay with them.

Physical product · Agriculture

KU Leuven: pest detection for growers

KU Leuven developed and patented an AI technology that detects pests in the field. The question was how that technology becomes a product growers can actually use: an app or a physical product, who uses it, and where it fits in the market. With user research, interviews, workshops and a creative session, we worked out that product, up to and including the product definition. KU Leuven received a client platform with all the information and the decision log.

Digital product · Training

Novare: an AI tool for learning transfer

Novare runs training programmes and wanted an AI tool to help people apply what they learn back at work. That asks for a different design than a regular product or learning platform. Together we defined what the tool does, for whom, and what goes in and what stays out.
