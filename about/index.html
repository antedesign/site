§ 01 · Origin About Anté

Why Anté exists.

Anté was founded by two product developers who saw the same issue repeat itself across innovation projects. Strong technical execution, but weak decisions before development started.

Too often, teams were forced to commit to one direction without enough certainty, alignment or objective input.

We started Anté to make innovations more independent, structured and evidence-based before major investments are made.

§ 02 · The position The Independence Contract

Independence is our business model.

Most agencies benefit when a project moves forward. That makes true objectivity difficult. Anté is deliberately structured to stay independent from what gets built.

Our role is simple: help teams build the right concept before a big investment is made. That is what allows us to stay objective.
