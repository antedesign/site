About Anté FILE · 03 / ABOUT · BELGIUM

We sell the decision, not the build.

Anté was founded by two product developers who watched the same pattern repeat across dozens of innovation projects: strong technical execution, weak conceptual foundation. The result was always the same: a product that worked, technically, but didn't solve the problem the team thought it was solving.

We started Anté to fix that pattern by inserting one missing step: a structured, evidence-based decision moment before the big investment.

An independent practice, based in Belgium, working across Europe. Decide before you build.
§ 01 · The position The Independence Contract

Why independence matters.

Most consultancies, design agencies, and engineering firms make their money once a build is decided. That creates a structural bias: keep the project moving, even when the concept underneath isn't ready.

We chose a different model. Anté gets paid for the decision, not the project that follows.

The moment we'd take referral fees, hold equity, or position ourselves as a preferred development partner, our recommendations become unreliable. So we've structurally ruled it out.

The Independence Contract · five commitments
01 We don't build what we recommend. ✓
02 We don't take referral fees from development partners. ✓
03 We don't hold equity in your company. ✓
04 We don't have affiliated builders, agencies, or engineering firms. ✓
05 We have no financial stake in what you decide to build. ✓
