§ 01 — The challenge Problem & fix
The problem

The decision just before the big investment is the riskiest moment.

Without a sharp, validated concept you end up building in the wrong direction — or worse, paralysed by indecision. Both cost money. The concept stage is where innovation projects fail, long before the build begins.

The fix

Anté builds the strategic foundation your decision needs.

A decision framework grounded in real stakeholder data, translated into visual concepts your team can debate and compare. No assumptions. No gut feel. Certainty — sourced and auditable.

§ 02 — How it works 4 phases · 21 days
1

Align

Synchronise all stakeholders, map existing knowledge, and define the strict concept boundaries before any direction is explored.

2

Explore

Translate the validated strategy into multiple visual directions and functional concepts — each linked to specific sub-problems.

3

Validate

The Anté tool replaces gut feeling with a data-driven trade-off. Every score is sourced. Every claim is auditable.

4

Handoff

A complete blueprint for the build phase: specifications, scope, open questions, and a quote-ready spec any engineering partner can pick up.
