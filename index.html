§ 03 · The problem Three failure modes

The decision before the build is where projects fail.

The riskiest moment in any innovation project isn't the build phase. It's the moment just before, when the concept is still vague, the team is split between three directions, and the budget is about to be committed.

At that moment most teams choose by enthusiasm, by who argues loudest, or by avoiding the choice altogether. The cost of that becomes visible six months later, after €100–500k has been spent.

Failure mode 01

Building the wrong thing

The team commits to one direction, ignores two others that would have worked better, and discovers it after the prototype is done.

Failure mode 02

Building the right thing wrong

The concept is sound but the specification is fuzzy. Engineering takes its best guess. The result solves a different problem than the one that mattered.

Failure mode 03

Hesitating until it's too late

The team can't agree, the decision keeps slipping, and a competitor moves first.

All three are decision problems, not build problems. Anté solves the decision.

§ 04 · The methodology 21 days · €12.500 · fixed

The 21-day Decision Sprint.

Four phases, fixed timeline, fixed price. From your vague idea to a substantiated choice your whole team can stand behind.

1
Phase 1 · Days 1–5

Listen

We interview your stakeholders and the people you're building for. Eight conversations, structured, recorded, mapped. The output is a synthesis dossier: every assumption you currently hold, mapped against actual evidence.

2
Phase 2 · Days 6–12

Translate

We turn the dossier into three fundamentally different concept directions. Visualised. Named. Made tangible enough to argue about. This is where most teams collapse to one direction prematurely. We resist that.

3
Phase 3 · Days 13–17

Decide

The trade-off matrix. Each direction scored on feasibility, business impact, and user value, every score traceable to specific evidence from phase one. No subjective ratings, no gut feeling, no "I think." A defensible decision your board can sign off on.

4
Phase 4 · Days 18–21

Hand over

A handover brief that any engineering firm or development team can pick up tomorrow. Specifications, scope, requirements, open questions. You enter the build phase knowing exactly what you're building and why.
