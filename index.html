AI Product Definition · Fictional example

BrewCare

An assistant in the coffee machine and the service app that warns office managers before the machine fails, and tells the technician what to bring.

For whom

  • User: the office manager who looks after the machines.
  • Second user: the company's own service technician.
  • Buyer: the facility or purchasing manager.

The problem

Machines stop at the worst moment. Service visits are reactive, and technicians often arrive without the right part. Customers judge the brand on downtime, not on coffee.

What the product does

  • Warns the office manager a few days before a likely problem, in plain language.
  • Gives the technician a likely cause and parts list before the visit.
  • Guides the office manager through simple fixes.

Role of AI

  • Does: predicts likely problems from usage and sensor data. Drafts the technician's brief.
  • Does not: decide on repairs, order parts, or chat freely with users.

Features, by priority

Must

Early warning with the reason. Technician brief. “Report a problem” button.

Should

Guided self-fix for the three most common issues. “This warning was wrong” feedback.

Later

Automatic parts ordering. Usage insights for the buyer.

Data it relies on

Usage logs from connected machines. Service history from the CRM, partly free text. No personal data from coffee drinkers.

Risks, and how we handle them

  • False warnings: shown as “likely”, with one-tap feedback.
  • Older machines without sensors: out of scope for version 1.
  • Messy service history: a clean-up step before the build.

Success criteria

  • Office managers understand a warning without help.
  • Technicians bring the right part more often than today.
  • Few warnings are marked wrong after three months.

Open questions

  • Will customers agree to share machine data?
  • Part of the service contract, or a paid add-on?
  • Which partner builds the prediction model?

Brief for the builder

Version 1: connected machines only, two user roles, the three must-have features, in the existing service app and on the machine display. Out of scope: parts ordering, open chat, older models.
