AI Product Definition · Fictional example

PackSight

A camera unit that checks every pack on the line, learns a customer's own defects from a few examples, and shows the operator what it found and why. Sold as a new product next to the machines.

For whom

  • User: the line operator and the quality lead at the customer.
  • Second user: the builder's service technician, who installs and tunes the unit.
  • Buyer: the plant manager at the customer.

What the product does

  • Checks every pack as it passes the camera.
  • Learns a new defect from about ten marked examples, without a programmer.
  • Shows each flagged pack with the reason highlighted, so the operator can accept or reject.

Role of AI

  • Does: recognises defects in images and learns new ones from examples. The product does not exist without it.
  • Does not: stop the line, change machine settings or decide what counts as a defect. The operator confirms.

Features, by priority

Must

Five common defects out of the box. Teach a new defect from examples. Flagged pack with the reason shown.

Should

Report per shift. “This flag was wrong” feedback.

Later

Automatic rejection through the line controller. Remote support.
