Our 21-day decision sprint.

A fixed 21-day system that turns early-stage uncertainty into a defensible concept direction. Most innovation projects fail not because of execution, but because the foundation underneath was never made explicit. Anté's methodology is structured to surface that foundation phase by phase, with the client involved at every checkpoint.

The methodology

Five phases, one spine.

0Working days
0Concept directions
0Dimensions
01

Context & Evidence

We map what is actually known, what is assumed and what is missing — through structured stakeholder interviews and targeted market input.

02

Problem Definition

We translate fragmented input into a single structured problem definition, including the criteria for what "good" looks like.

03

Concept Exploration

From one well-defined problem we develop three fundamentally different concept directions — strategic alternatives, not variations.

04

Decision Matrix

Every trade-off is linked back to evidence from each prior phase. What remains is traceable reasoning, not preference.

05

Implementation Brief

The chosen direction becomes a structured brief engineering or partners can act on immediately — scope, requirements, constraints.
